6. Interpretation and Limitations

MAE and RMSE measure prediction error; lower values are better. R-squared measures explained variation; higher values are generally better. Feature coefficients show associations within this fitted model and do not establish causation.

Important: This project uses a synthetic academic dataset. The predictions are illustrative only, not official admission probabilities, guarantees, or a substitute for institution-specific admission criteria.
